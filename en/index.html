About the sport

What is F3C?

F3C is a radio-controlled helicopter class focused on precision and technique. It's a competition class in which pilots perform a set of predetermined manoeuvres that are judged on accuracy and style.

F3C helicopters are often larger and more advanced than other models, with features that enable stable flight and precise control. Competition manoeuvres can include things like hovering, pirouettes and various turns, and are judged by referees on criteria such as timing, technique and execution.

We are the part of SMFF that runs competition in radio-controlled helicopters. We welcome new and experienced competition pilots alike. Under the tabs above you can read about various activities, events and more. There is also a tab for beginners who would like to try competing, or ease into it gradually.

Why not already take a look under the FAI Rulebook and try a manoeuvre from F3C POP (Beginner), F3C Sport (Intermediate), or F3C FAI (Official competition class). We're now looking forward to a fantastic 2027 competition season!
